Customizing the 'Popular Posts' Widget

The 'Popular Posts' widget is one of the most effective ways to keep visitors on your blog by showing them your most successful content. However, the default look can be a bit plain. Let's learn how to customize it.

1. The Basic Widget

You can add the widget via Layout > Add a Gadget > Popular Posts. It allows you to show snippets, thumbnails, and filter by time range (Last 30 days, All time, etc.).

2. Custom HTML Structure

To fully customize the look, you need to edit the b:widget code. You can use resizeImage to ensure thumbnails are uniform and snippet to control the text length.

<b:loop values='data:posts' var='post'>
  <div class='popular-item'>
    <img expr:src='resizeImage(data:post.featuredImage, 100, "1:1")'/>
    <a expr:href='data:post.url'><data:post.title/></a>
  </div>
</b:loop>

3. Styling with CSS

Use CSS styling to create a grid or a list with hover effects. For professional designs, many developers use the blogger_theme framework to manage complex widget layouts in separate files.

A well-designed Popular Posts section can significantly reduce your bounce rate and improve your SEO by increasing internal linking.
