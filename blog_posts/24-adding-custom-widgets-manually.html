Adding Custom Widgets Manually

While Blogger provides many built-in gadgets (Labels, Archive, Profile), sometimes you need something unique—like a custom social media feed, a weather widget, or a specialized navigation menu.

1. The "HTML/JavaScript" Gadget

The easiest way to add custom code is using the built-in HTML/JavaScript gadget:

  1. Go to Layout.
  2. Click Add a Gadget in your sidebar or footer.
  3. Select HTML/JavaScript.
  4. Paste your custom code (HTML, CSS, or JS) into the box and save.

2. Hard-Coding into the Template

For more control, you can write the widget directly into your theme's XML. This is better for widgets that need to interact with Blogger data.

<b:widget id='CustomAboutMe' title='About the Author' type='HTML'>
  <b:includable id='main'>
    <div class='widget-content'>
      <img src='author-photo.jpg' />
      <p>Hi! I am the author of this blog...</p>
    </div>
  </b:includable>
</b:widget>

3. Using External Widgets

Many services (like Elfsight, Powr, or even Twitter) provide "embed codes." These are usually just small snippets of JavaScript. Always remember to place these at the end of your widget or even just before the </body> tag of your template to prevent them from slowing down your page load.

Custom widgets allow you to extend Blogger's functionality far beyond its default settings.
