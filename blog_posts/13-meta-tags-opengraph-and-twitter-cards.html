Meta Tags, OpenGraph, and Twitter Cards

Meta tags are snippets of text that describe a page's content to search engines and social media. Mastering these is key to Advanced SEO. For a deep dive, see What is Meta Tags and Opengraph Tag?.

1. Social Sharing

  • OpenGraph (OG): Controls how links look on Facebook and LinkedIn.
  • Twitter Cards: Enhances sharing on Twitter. Learn how to make a Twitter Card for Blogger.

2. Dynamic Implementation

In your theme's <head>, you can use Blogger Operators and Data Tags to automatically pull information for each post. See our guide on Dynamically creating Meta Tags.

<meta property="og:title" expr:content='data:blog.pageName' />
<meta property="og:image" expr:content='data:post.featuredImage' />

3. Connection to Structured Data

While meta tags are for humans and social platforms, Structured Data (JSON-LD) is for search engines to create rich snippets. Both are essential for a professional blog.

Proper meta tags ensure that your content looks professional everywhere it's shared, leading to more clicks and higher engagement.
