Meta Tags, OpenGraph, and Twitter Cards

Meta tags are snippets of text that describe a page's content. They don't appear on the page itself, but in the page's code. They are essential for SEO and for how your blog posts look when shared on social media.

1. Standard Meta Tags

These tell search engines about your blog. Most are handled automatically by Blogger, but you can customize them in Settings > Meta tags.

  • Description: A summary of your post that appears in search results.
  • Keywords: Less important today, but still used by some smaller search engines.

2. OpenGraph Tags (Facebook/LinkedIn)

OpenGraph (OG) tags control how your links look when shared on Facebook and LinkedIn. Without them, Facebook might pick a random image from your blog or no image at all.

<meta property="og:title" expr:content='data:blog.pageName' />
<meta property="og:type" content="article" />
<meta property="og:url" expr:content='data:blog.url' />
<meta property="og:image" expr:content='data:post.featuredImage' />

3. Twitter Cards

Similar to OpenGraph, Twitter Cards make your links look like rich media on Twitter, including a large image, title, and description.

<meta name="twitter:card" content="summary_large_image" />
<meta name="twitter:title" expr:content='data:blog.pageName' />
<meta name="twitter:image" expr:content='data:post.featuredImage' />

How to add them dynamically?

Blogger now includes many of these automatically if you use the <b:include data='blog' name='all-head-content'/> tag in your theme's head section. However, for full control, you can manually insert them using Blogger expressions to pull the post title and featured image dynamically.

Proper meta tags ensure that your content looks professional everywhere it's shared, leading to more clicks and higher engagement.
