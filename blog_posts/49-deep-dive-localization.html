Deep Dive: b:message and b:param (Localization)

If you want to build themes that support multiple languages, you need to understand <b:message> and <b:param>. These tags allow you to pull translated strings directly from Blogger's servers. Learn more at BloggerBasics101.

1. The b:message Tag

Fetches a predefined message based on the blog's language setting. For example, the "Read More" text.

<b:message name='messages.readMore'/>

2. The b:param Tag

Some messages require parameters (like "Showing 5 of 10 posts"). You use b:param to fill in those blanks.

3. Why use them?

  • Accessibility: Your blog automatically speaks the user's language.
  • Consistency: Uses the same terminology as the Blogger dashboard.
  • Professionalism: Essential for themes designed for global distribution or custom domain use.

Combined with Conditional Tags, you can create a truly localized User Experience for readers all over the world.
