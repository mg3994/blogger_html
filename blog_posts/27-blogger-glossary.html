Blogger Glossary: SERP, Crawling, and Indexing

To master Blogger, you need to speak the language of the web. Here are the most important terms you'll encounter as you grow your blog.

1. SERP (Search Engine Results Page)

SERP is the page that appears after you search for something on Google. It's the list of results that Google thinks are most relevant to your query. The goal of SEO is to get your blog posts onto the first page of the SERPs.

2. Crawling

Crawling is the process where search engines send out "bots" (like Googlebot) to discover new and updated content. They follow links from one page to another. This is why having a good internal linking structure and a sitemap is so important.

3. Indexing

After a bot crawls your page, it tries to understand what the page is about. This process is called indexing. If the page is high quality, it is added to Google's massive "index"—a giant library of the web. If your page isn't indexed, it won't show up in search results.

4. User Agent (UA)

A User Agent is a string of text that identifies your browser and operating system to the website you are visiting. Blogger uses this to decide whether to show you the desktop or mobile version of the blog.

5. Permalinks

A permalink is the permanent URL of an individual blog post. In Blogger, these usually look like /year/month/post-title.html. You can customize these in the post settings before you publish.

6. CDATA

In your XML template, you'll often see <![CDATA[ ... ]]>. This tells the Blogger compiler: "Ignore everything inside these brackets; it's just raw code (CSS or JS)." This prevents the compiler from getting confused by symbols like < or &.

Understanding these terms will help you better understand the technical side of blogging and SEO.
