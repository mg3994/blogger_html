Customizing UX: 404 Pages, Mobile m=1, and GDPR

A great blog isn't just about content; it's about the User Experience (UX). Small technical tweaks can make a huge difference.

1. Creating a Custom 404 Page

Don't let broken links frustrate your visitors. Learn how to Create a 404 Custom Page with a Search Box.

2. The Mobile m=1 Parameter

Blogger adds ?m=1 to mobile URLs. If you are using a modern responsive theme, you might want to Disable the Mobile View Parameter to keep your Meta Tag URLs clean.

3. GDPR and Cookie Notifications

Compliance is mandatory for many blogs. Understand What is GDPR and how it impacts your blog. You can also Customize the Blogger Cookies Notification Bar using CSS styling.

Focusing on these UX elements, along with Breadcrumbs and Performance, will make your blog stand out as a professional site.
