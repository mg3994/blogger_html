Blogger Operators Guide

In programming, an operator is a character that tells the compiler to perform a specific action, such as mathematical calculations or logical comparisons. Blogger has its own set of operators used within its XML template language.

1. Arithmetic Operators

Used for mathematical calculations:

  • Addition: +
  • Subtraction: -
  • Multiplication: *
  • Division: /
  • Modulo: % (returns the remainder of a division)

2. Comparison Operators

Used to compare two values, often within b:if statements:

  • Equality: == or eq
  • Inequality: != or neq
  • Less Than: < or lt
  • Greater Than: > or gt
  • Less Than or Equal: <= or lte
  • Greater Than or Equal: >= or gte

3. Logical Operators

Used to combine multiple boolean expressions:

  • AND: && or and
  • OR: || or or
  • NOT: ! or not

4. Lambda Operators

Advanced operators used with collections (arrays):

  • any, all, none, filter, first, map, count.
  • The lambda symbol is =>.

5. Special Blogger Operators

  • Concatenate (+): Joins two strings together.
  • Ternary (? :): A shorthand if/else statement (e.g., condition ? trueValue : falseValue).
  • Membership (in, contains): Checks if a value exists in a collection or string.
  • snippet: Shortens a string of text to a specific length.
  • resizeImage: Automatically resizes images hosted on Google servers.
