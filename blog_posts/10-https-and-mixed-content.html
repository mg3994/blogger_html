Security First: HTTPS and Fixing Mixed Content

Security is no longer optional. Google prioritizes secure websites, and visitors feel safer browsing them. Blogger provides free SSL certificates for all blogs.

1. Enabling HTTPS on Blogger

To turn on HTTPS for your blog:

  1. Go to your Blogger dashboard.
  2. Select Settings.
  3. Scroll down to the HTTPS section.
  4. Toggle HTTPS availability to "Yes".
  5. Toggle HTTPS redirect to "Yes" (this ensures all http:// traffic is moved to https://).

2. What is Mixed Content?

Mixed content occurs when a secure (HTTPS) page includes resources (images, scripts, styles) that are being loaded via an insecure (HTTP) connection. This creates a security hole and often results in a "Not Secure" warning in the browser address bar.

3. How to Fix Mixed Content

If you see a mixed content warning, you need to find the http:// links in your template or posts and change them to https://.

Common Culprits:

  • Images hosted on external sites.
  • External scripts or CSS libraries.
  • iFrames (like old YouTube embed codes).

The Easy Fix:

In most cases, you can simply open your theme's HTML editor, press Ctrl+F, search for http://, and manually update the links to https://. If the external site supports HTTPS, the resource will load perfectly.

Keeping your blog secure protects your visitors and improves your SEO standing.
