Security First: HTTPS and Fixing Mixed Content

Security is no longer optional. Google prioritizes secure websites, and visitors feel safer browsing them. For a deeper understanding of web security, see Secure Websites and SSL/TLS Certificates.

1. Enabling HTTPS on Blogger

Blogger provides free SSL for all blogs. You can activate it in Settings > HTTPS. Learn the full steps to turn on HTTPS here.

2. What is Mixed Content?

Mixed content occurs when a secure (HTTPS) page includes resources (images, scripts, styles) loaded via http://. This can break your CSS styling or show security warnings. Read more about Mixed Content types.

3. How to Fix Mixed Content

If you see a mixed content warning, you must update your links to https://. For blogs with hundreds of posts, you might need a search and replace tool.

Common Culprits:

  • Images from external sites (host them on Blogger/Google instead).
  • External Performance scripts.

Keeping your blog secure protects your visitors and improves your SEO standing, especially when combined with proper Meta Tags.
