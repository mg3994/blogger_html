Mastering the b:loop Tag

The <b:loop> tag is used to iterate through a collection of Array data. It's the primary way Blogger displays your post list. See the full b:loop documentation for more.

Basic Syntax

<b:loop values='DATA_COLLECTION' var='VARIABLE_NAME'>
  <!-- Content to repeat -->
</b:loop>

Filtering Loops with Lambda

You can combine loops with Lambda Operators for powerful filtering:

<!-- Only show the first 3 posts -->
<b:loop values='data:posts limit 3' var='post'>
  <h2><data:post.title/></h2>
</b:loop>

This is a great technique for optimizing mobile views.

Loops are also critical for generating Post Label Summaries. Understanding how to manage your var and values Attributes is the key to success.
