Integrating Disqus or Facebook Comments

While Blogger's built-in comment system is reliable, third-party systems like Disqus or Facebook Comments offer advanced features like social login, image uploads, and better spam protection.

1. Why Switch?

  • Engagement: Social logins make it easier for people to comment.
  • Moderation: Advanced tools for handling spam and trolls.
  • Real-time: Comments appear instantly without page refreshes.

2. How to Install Disqus

  1. Create a Disqus account and add your blog's URL.
  2. Blogger provides a one-click install, or you can manually add the code.
  3. Use Conditional Tags to ensure the comments only load on Post Pages and not on your homepage.

3. Impact on Performance

Third-party scripts can slow down your blog. To maintain high performance, consider "lazy loading" the comments so they only load when a user scrolls to the bottom of the page.

Choosing the right comment system is an important part of your User Experience strategy.
