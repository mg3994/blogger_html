Blogger Knowledge: Search Console and Sitemaps

Creating great content is only half the battle. To ensure people actually find your blog, you need to understand how search engines interact with your site. For more technical details, check the Search Console Guide on BloggerBasics101.

1. What is Google Search Console?

Google Search Console (GSC) is a free service that helps you monitor, maintain, and troubleshoot your blog's presence in Google Search results. It's a critical part of Advanced SEO for Blogger.

2. Understanding Sitemaps

A sitemap is a file (usually sitemap.xml) that contains all the URLs of your blog. Learn more about Blogger Sitemaps here.

Where is my Blogger sitemap?

Blogger automatically generates a sitemap for you. You can find it at:

https://yourblog.blogspot.com/sitemap.xml

3. How to Submit your Sitemap to GSC

  1. Sign in to Google Search Console.
  2. Add your blog as a property and verify ownership.
  3. Go to the Sitemaps section and submit sitemap.xml.

4. Crawl and Index

Understanding the difference between Crawling and Indexing is essential for diagnosing why your posts might not be showing up in search results. You can control this behavior using Robots.txt.

By properly using Search Console and sitemaps, you are giving your blog the best possible chance to rank high in search results alongside your Structured Data.
