Blogger Knowledge: Search Console and Sitemaps

Creating great content is only half the battle. To ensure people actually find your blog, you need to understand how search engines interact with your site.

1. What is Google Search Console?

Google Search Console (GSC) is a free service that helps you monitor, maintain, and troubleshoot your blog's presence in Google Search results. It allows you to:

  • Confirm that Google can find and crawl your site.
  • Fix indexing problems and request re-indexing of new content.
  • View Google Search traffic data (keywords, clicks, impressions).
  • Receive alerts when Google encounters indexing, spam, or other issues on your site.

2. Understanding Sitemaps

A sitemap is a file (usually sitemap.xml) where you provide information about the pages, videos, and other files on your site, and the relationships between them. Search engines like Google read this file to more intelligently crawl your site.

Where is my Blogger sitemap?

Blogger automatically generates a sitemap for you. You can find it by adding /sitemap.xml to your blog URL:

https://yourblog.blogspot.com/sitemap.xml

3. How to Submit your Sitemap to GSC

  1. Sign in to Google Search Console.
  2. Select your blog property.
  3. On the left menu, click Sitemaps.
  4. In the 'Add a new sitemap' section, type sitemap.xml.
  5. Click Submit.

4. Crawling vs. Indexing

  • Crawling: The process by which Googlebot discovers new and updated pages to be added to the Google index.
  • Indexing: The process by which Googlebot processes each of the pages it crawls in order to compile a massive index of all the words it sees and their location on each page.

By properly using Search Console and sitemaps, you are giving your blog the best possible chance to rank high in search results.
