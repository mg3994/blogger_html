Limiting Posts on Mobile View

If you have a very content-heavy blog, your mobile users might struggle with long loading times. You can set a different limit for how many posts appear on your homepage when viewed on a mobile device.

1. Why limit mobile posts?

  • Speed: Fewer posts mean fewer images and scripts to load.
  • User Experience: Prevents "infinite scrolling" fatigue on small screens.
  • Data Usage: Saves bandwidth for your mobile visitors.

2. The Code Trick

In your Blog1 widget, Blogger uses a loop to display posts. You can use a conditional check on data:view.isMobile combined with the limit operator.

<!-- In your Blog1 widget's main includable -->
<b:with value='data:view.isMobile ? 3 : 7' var='postLimit'>
  <b:loop values='data:posts limit data:postLimit' var='post'>
    <!-- Display post content -->
  </b:loop>
</b:with>

In this example, the blog will show 3 posts on mobile and 7 posts on desktop.

3. Blogger's "Mobile Theme" Settings

If you are using an older, non-responsive theme, you can also go to Theme > Mobile and choose how your blog looks. However, for modern themes, the "Responsive" setting is always recommended, and the code-based limit above gives you much more control.
