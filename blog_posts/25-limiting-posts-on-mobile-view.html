Limiting Posts on Mobile View

Performance on mobile is critical for SEO. One simple trick is to Limit the Number of Posts Displayed in Mobile View.

1. Why Limit?

Mobile users often have slower connections. Reducing the number of Featured Images and post bodies loaded can significantly improve Performance. It also creates a better User Experience on small screens.

2. Implementation

Use Conditional Tags and the limit Operator in your b:loop:

<b:loop values='data:posts limit (data:view.isMobile ? 3 : 7)' var='post'> ... </b:loop>

3. SEO and UX

Fast mobile pages are prioritized in the SERPs. Combine this with Post Summaries and Breadcrumbs to create the ultimate mobile-friendly blog.

Performance optimization for mobile is no longer a luxury; it's a necessity for modern professional blogging.
