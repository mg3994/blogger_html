Adding a 'Related Posts' section using Labels

A 'Related Posts' section at the end of every article encourages readers to stay on your blog. Since Blogger doesn't have this feature built-in, we use Labels and some JavaScript to create one.

1. The Logic

We use a script that looks at the Array of labels for the current post and then fetches other posts with the same labels using the Blogger Feed API.

2. Template Integration

You need to place the script container inside your Blog1 widget, usually just after the post body.

<b:if cond='data:view.isPost'>
  <div id='related-posts'/>
  <script src='/path/to/related-posts.js'/>
</b:if>

3. Styling

Use CSS to style the thumbnails and titles. Many designers use the resizeImage operator within their feed scripts to ensure the related post thumbnails load fast and look great.

Related posts are a cornerstone of Advanced SEO, as they improve your site's internal link structure and session duration.
