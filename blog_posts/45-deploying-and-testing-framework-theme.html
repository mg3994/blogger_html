Deploying and Testing your Framework Theme

Congratulations! You've moved from Starting your Blogger Journey to building professional themes with a Modern Framework.

1. The Terminal Step

Before you finalize your theme, you must test it across all Blogger Views. Ensure your Real Homepage, post pages, and Search pages all look perfect.

2. Cross-Device Verification

Use your browser's mobile emulator to verify Mobile Performance. Check that your Breadcrumbs and Social Buttons are easy to tap on small screens.

3. Final SEO Check

Run your blog through Google Search Console to ensure your Structured Data and Meta Tags are being read correctly by Googlebot.
