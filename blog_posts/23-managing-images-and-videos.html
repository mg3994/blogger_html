Managing Uploaded Images and Videos

Proper media management is key to Performance Optimization and organization. For full details, see How to Manage Uploaded Images or Video.

1. Storage and SEO

Images are stored in Google Album Archive. For better SEO, always add Alt Text and Title Text to your images. This helps bots crawl and index your media correctly.

2. Image Manipulation

Don't upload massive files! Use the resizeImage Operator to serve perfectly sized thumbnails for your Featured Images. This is critical for mobile performance.

3. Video Integration

While you can upload directly, embedding from YouTube is often better for Page Speed. YouTube videos can also be automatically resized using Blogger operators.

Organized media management ensures that your blog remains fast and that your content is accessible to everyone, regardless of their device.
