Managing Uploaded Images and Videos

Every image and video you upload to Blogger is stored in your Google Account. Knowing how to manage these assets is key to keeping your blog organized and within storage limits.

1. Where are my images stored?

Images uploaded to Blogger are stored in the Google Album Archive (and often visible in Google Photos). When you delete an image from a post, it does not automatically delete it from your storage. You must go to the archive to remove it permanently.

2. Image Alt Text and Title Text

For SEO and accessibility, always add Alt Text to your images:

  1. Click on the image in the Blogger editor.
  2. Click the Settings (gear) icon.
  3. Enter Alt text (describes the image for screen readers and bots) and Title text (shows when a user hovers over the image).

3. Video Management

Blogger allows you to upload videos directly or embed them from YouTube.

  • Direct Upload: Stored in your blog's "Videos" section in settings. There is a limit on direct uploads, so YouTube is usually preferred.
  • YouTube Embed: The best way for performance. It doesn't use your Blogger storage and allows for better playback controls.

4. Managing Storage Quotas

Blogger storage is shared with your Google Drive and Gmail. If you run out of space, you may need to delete old, unused images from the Album Archive or upgrade your Google One plan.

Organized media management ensures that your blog remains fast and that your images always show up correctly for your readers.
