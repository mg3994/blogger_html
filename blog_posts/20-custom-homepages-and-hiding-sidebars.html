Custom Homepages and Hiding Sidebars

Advanced layouts allow you to break free from the standard Blogger look. Learn how to Create a Real Homepage for your Blogger Blog.

1. Hiding Sidebars

You can use Conditional Tags to hide specific Sections on certain pages. See the tutorial on How to Hide Sidebar on Certain Pages.

<b:if cond='!data:view.isPage'>
  <b:section id='sidebar'> ... </b:section>
</b:if>

2. Styling with CSS

When you hide elements, you'll need to adjust your layout using CSS. Use the Inspect Tool to find the right selectors and apply full-width styles to your content area.

Combining these techniques with Breadcrumbs and Structured Data will give your blog a truly professional, custom-built feel.
