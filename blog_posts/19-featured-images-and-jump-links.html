Featured Images and the 'Jump Link'

How you structure your individual posts affects how they look on your homepage and in search results.

1. The Importance of Featured Images

Blogger doesn't have a "Set Featured Image" button like WordPress. Instead, it usually picks the first image in your post as the thumbnail for your homepage and social media shares.

Pro Tip: If you want a specific image to be the thumbnail but don't want it to appear at the very top of your post, you can use CSS to hide it while still allowing Blogger to "see" it as the first image.

2. The 'Jump Link' (Read More)

Have you ever seen a blog where the entire post is displayed on the homepage? It's messy and bad for SEO. The "Jump Link" (or Jump Break) allows you to show only a snippet of your post on the main page with a "Read More" link.

How to insert a Jump Link:

  1. While writing a post, click the Insert Jump Break icon in the toolbar (it looks like a dashed line).
  2. Blogger inserts <!--more--> into your HTML.
  3. Everything above this line appears on your homepage. Everything below is hidden until the user clicks the post.

3. Why use Jump Links?

  • Faster Loading: Your homepage loads much faster because it's not loading every image and paragraph from every post.
  • Better Analytics: It forces users to click through to the post, giving you more accurate "Page View" data.
  • Professional Look: Creates a clean, consistent grid of snippets on your homepage.

Consistent use of jump links and high-quality first images will instantly make your blog look more professional.
