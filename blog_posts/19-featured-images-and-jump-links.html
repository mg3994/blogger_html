Featured Images and the 'Jump Link'

The structure of your posts affects how they look on your homepage and social media. See What is Featured Images? for more context.

1. Featured Images

Blogger uses the first image in your post as the thumbnail. This image is also used for your OpenGraph and Twitter Card Meta Tags. Always ensure it is high quality!
