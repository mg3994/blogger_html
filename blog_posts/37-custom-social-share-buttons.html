Building Custom Social Share Buttons

Social sharing is vital for your blog's growth. While many widgets exist, building your own custom buttons allows you to maintain perfect CSS styling and Performance.

1. The Share URLs

Each social platform has a specific URL structure for sharing. For example:

  • Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/sharer.xml?u=[URL]
  • Twitter: https://twitter.com/intent/tweet?url=[URL]&text=[TITLE]

2. Implementation with Blogger Tags

Use expr:href to dynamically insert the post URL and title into these share links:

<a expr:href='"https://twitter.com/intent/tweet?url=" + data:post.url + "&text=" + data:post.title' target='_blank'>
  Share on Twitter
</a>

3. Optimization

Custom buttons don't require heavy external JavaScript libraries, which keeps your page load speed fast. You can also add counts or icons using custom widgets. Combined with proper Meta Tags (OpenGraph), your content will look amazing whenever it's shared.

Building your own tools gives you the power to create a truly unique User Experience for your readers.
