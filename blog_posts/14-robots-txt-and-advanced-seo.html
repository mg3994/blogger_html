Robots.txt and Advanced SEO for Blogger

To have full control over how search engines crawl your blog, you need to understand the robots.txt file and robots meta tags. For more information, read What is Web Robot and Robots.txt?.

1. What is Robots.txt?

It's a file that tells search engine crawlers which parts of your blog to ignore. You can find your blog's version at /robots.txt.

2. Custom Robots Header Tags

Blogger allows you to set custom header tags in Settings. This is a powerful way to tell robots not to index specific types of pages, like Archives. This helps prevent "duplicate content" issues in the SERPs.

3. SEO Connections

Advanced SEO involves combining these crawling rules with Sitemaps, Meta Tags, and Structured Data. Together, they make your blog highly visible to search engines like Google.

Always remember to test your SEO changes using Google Search Console to ensure your blog is being crawled correctly.
