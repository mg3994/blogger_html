Robots.txt and Advanced SEO for Blogger

To have full control over how search engines crawl your blog, you need to understand the robots.txt file and how to use robots meta tags.

1. What is Robots.txt?

The robots.txt file tells search engine crawlers which pages or files the crawler can or can't request from your site. This is used mainly to avoid overloading your site with requests.

Blogger's Default Robots.txt

By default, Blogger creates a perfect robots.txt for you. It allows crawling of posts and pages but blocks search result pages (to avoid duplicate content issues).

2. Customizing Robots.txt

You can edit this in Settings > Crawlers and indexing > Custom robots.txt. Warning: Improper settings can hide your entire blog from Google!

3. Custom Robots Header Tags

This is a safer and more precise way to control indexing. In Settings, you can set defaults for:

  • Home page: Usually set to all.
  • Archive and Search Pages: Usually set to noindex to avoid duplicate content.
  • Posts and Pages: Set to all.

4. Blocking Specific Pages (noindex)

If you have a specific post you don't want to show in search results, you can add this inside your template within a conditional tag:

<b:if cond='data:blog.url == "URL_OF_PAGE_TO_HIDE"'>
  <meta name="robots" content="noindex" />
</b:if>

Advanced SEO is about making it as easy as possible for Google to find your high-quality content while hiding the technical or duplicate "noise" of your blog.
