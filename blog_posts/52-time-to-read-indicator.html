Blogger Trick: Implementing a "Time to Read" Indicator

Letting your readers know how long a post will take to read can increase engagement and improve your User Experience. You can calculate this dynamically using JavaScript and Blogger Data.

1. The Concept

The average person reads about 200-250 words per minute. We'll take your post body, count the words, and display the result.

2. The Template Code

Place this inside your Blog1 Widget, near the post title or Breadcrumbs.

<b:if cond='data:view.isPost'>
  <span id='reading-time'></span>
</b:if>

3. The Script

<script>
  var text = document.querySelector('.post-body').innerText;
  var wpm = 225;
  var words = text.trim().split(/\s+/).length;
  var time = Math.ceil(words / wpm);
  document.getElementById('reading-time').innerText = time + ' min read';
</script>

4. SEO and Performance

This small addition doesn't affect Performance much but makes your blog feel modern. Combine it with Structured Data to give both humans and bots a better understanding of your content.
