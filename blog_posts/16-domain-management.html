Domain Management: Custom Domains and Subdomains

Moving to a custom domain is a major step in professionalizing your blog. Read What is a Custom Domain? to understand the benefits.

1. Setting up a Custom Domain

The process involves updating your DNS settings at your registrar. Follow our Step-by-Step Custom Domain Setup guide. Don't forget to activate HTTPS once your domain is ready!

2. Using Subdomains

If you have multiple blogs, you can use Subdomains for Multiple Blogspot Blogs (e.g., news.yoursite.com).

3. SEO Impact

A custom domain improves your brand trust and SEO. Make sure to update your property in Google Search Console and re-submit your Sitemap after the move.

A custom domain is an investment in your brand's identity and longevity on the web.
