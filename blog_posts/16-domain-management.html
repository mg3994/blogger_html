Domain Management: Custom Domains and Subdomains

Moving from a .blogspot.com address to a custom domain (like www.yourname.com) is one of the best ways to make your blog look professional.

1. Setting up a Custom Domain

  1. Buy a domain from a registrar (Google Domains, Namecheap, GoDaddy).
  2. In Blogger, go to Settings > Publishing > Custom domain.
  3. Enter your domain (include the www).
  4. Blogger will provide two CNAME records. You must add these to your domain registrar's DNS settings.
  5. Add four A-records pointing to Google's IPs (216.239.32.21, 216.239.34.21, 216.239.36.21, 216.239.38.21).
  6. Wait for DNS propagation (can take up to 24 hours).

2. Setting up Subdomains

If you already have a custom domain for your main website, you can use a subdomain for your blog (e.g., blog.yourwebsite.com).

  • The process is the same as above, but instead of www, you use your chosen subdomain (e.g., blog) in the CNAME record.

3. Redirecting Naked Domains

Ensure that visitors who type yourname.com (without the www) still reach your blog. In Blogger settings, check the Redirect domain option after your custom domain is verified.

A custom domain is an investment in your brand's identity and longevity on the web.
